Kotone is a tiny lyrics player for the Mac. The songs you love come with furigana and a Chinese translation side by side, and any line opens up into its phrases, its words and its grammar.

Read along

Lyrics that follow the song

Each line lights up as it's sung, with its reading above it and its meaning beside it. Turn the furigana or the translation off when you're ready to go without.

Kotone plays through the Spotify app on your Mac and never pulls it to the front: you stay in the lyrics. A song that isn't on Spotify can use an audio file of your own.

Take it apart

Every line, word by word

Open any line to see how it's built, explained for that line, not in general.

  • Phrases matched to the Chinese, color by color, so you see which part means what.
  • Each word's reading and part of speech, its JLPT level, and how it's conjugated from its dictionary form, one step at a time.
  • The grammar points it uses, with a note on how they work right here.
Annotation

Written by your Codex, checked by Kotone

Add a song and ask your own Codex to annotate it. It runs on your ChatGPT plan, several lines at a time, and each line shows up as soon as it passes the check.

The check is not another model. Kotone re-derives every reading, every conjugation and every link to the Chinese from what Codex wrote, and anything that doesn't add up goes back to be fixed.

✓溶けるて形→ 溶けて matches the lyric
✓会うた形→ 会った reading あった
✓奪うた形→ 奪った godan, う → った
✗呼ぶ marked ichidan, but an ichidan verb ends in る
↻sent back to Codex to fix
Grammar outline

From a grammar point to your songs

Every grammar point you meet is collected in one outline, with the lines in your own songs that use it. Save the lines you like, and look words up by JLPT level.

Add a song

One search, and it's in

Search for a song and Kotone finds it on Spotify, with time-synced lyrics from four sources and its cover. Only the original Japanese is kept: the Chinese is written fresh for you, never copied from a lyrics site.
