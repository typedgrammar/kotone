Kotone 言音: learn Japanese through music. A tiny macOS lyrics player for Chinese-speaking learners at N5–N4. Lyrics come with furigana and a side-by-side Chinese translation, and any line opens into a breakdown (phrases matched across the two languages, each word's reading and conjugation, the grammar it uses).

- **Kotone-x.y.z.zip** (about 550 KB) or **.dmg**: move Kotone to Applications. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
- Needs macOS 13 or later on Apple silicon.
- Plays through the Spotify desktop app. macOS asks once whether Kotone may control it.
- Annotating in the app uses your own [Codex](https://github.com/openai/codex) CLI and ChatGPT plan.

See the [README](https://github.com/typedgrammar/kotone#readme) for everything else.
